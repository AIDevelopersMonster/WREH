# WR-V v0.1 — proposed scientific review scope

Work: *Experiment and Realization Selection: Branch, Contextualize, Select, or Remain a Class*. Author: A. A. Malachevsky, ORCID 0009-0008-6009-3196. Status: REVIEWABLE_DRAFT; no external peer review or WR-V DOI. This file prepares a review request; no request has been sent.

Please assess the common bilingual source and numbered proofs, rather than importing an audit verdict from an earlier WREH paper. The contribution proposed here is an experimental transition interface and a reproducible finite benchmark. Mathematical priority for the elementary marginalization, rank, feasible-direction and Bayes arguments is not claimed.

Questions for review:

1. Are input hypotheses, successor labels, transition laws and complete global realizations kept distinct throughout?
2. Are the product-of-simplices factorization, uniform rank criterion, pointwise feasible-direction criterion and row-space functional criterion correct under their explicit assumptions? Does any result silently require a stronger calibration, support or admissibility assumption?
3. Does the three-state benchmark correctly distinguish exact identification, mixture ambiguity, boundary exceptions and finite-resolution uncertainty? Are the error bounds and feasibility conditions sufficient for their stated purpose?
4. Does the abstract contextual triangle avoid claiming a physical implementation, and does the finite adaptive lemma account for protocol selection without covert hypothesis information?
5. Does the new instrument-completion carrier meet WR-V's distinct obligation without reopening WR-I–IV? Which closer prior art should a problem-specific novelty audit inspect?

Report counterexamples and missing assumptions first. Separate established mathematics, programme-specific synthesis and possible future physical claims. A negative outcome-only identification result is acceptable. Do not broaden a claim to quantum collapse, ontological branching or universal impossibility without an independently specified model and proof.

The technical audit and primary-source preflight are supplied as evidence of preparation, not as instructions to approve the manuscript. Stronger release status requires resolution of substantive review findings and a concrete novelty claim with a tailored literature check.
