## Obligation and source scope

The founding WREH-00 manifesto explicitly assigns WR-V the four realization scenarios. The new technical obligation is to distinguish an outcome marginal from a post-experiment transition model and determine what extra calibrated responses constrain that transition. The draft does not assume the four labels are a disjoint physical taxonomy.

## Anti-duplication map

| WR-V object | Inherited owner | Additional obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument marginal L versus transition K | Classical instrument theory; WR-IV set/law type discipline | Declare both carriers and the observable information that does or does not recover K |
| Instrument-completion class | WR-I–III response/fibre language | New carrier of experimental transitions, not a new general fibre theorem |
| Uniform rank and pointwise support certificate | Elementary linear/convex algebra; nonnegative inverse-problem literature | Auditable finite experiment benchmark, with no priority claim |
| Context-variable splitting | Established contextuality framework; WR-II extension discipline | Show that removing overlap identities changes the model rather than solving the original model |
| Positive finite adaptive likelihood | Elementary Bayes conditioning; WR-IV positivity distinction | Persistent hypotheses and adaptive records explicitly specified |

Acceptable negative result: immediate outcome statistics do not determine a declared successor model. Failure criterion: a four-scenario claim that mixes carrier types, infers physical branching, or asserts a new generic quotient/gluing result must be removed or narrowed. Downstream handoff: explicitly constrained admissible instrument completions; physical constraints require independent construction.
