## Anti-duplication result

WR-IV is not allowed to become another WR-II paper about whether a global completion exists, another WR-III paper about generic fibre geometry, or a renaming of inverse-limit / natural-extension theory.

The genuinely new WREH target is the historical projection of a response-conditioned global completion class:

\[\mathfrak H_-(D)=\pi_-(\mathfrak C(D)),\]

plus the distinction between hidden-past and observable-past ambiguity.

## Confirmed neighboring prior art

1. Natural extensions / inverse limits in dynamics: standard inverse-limit constructions replace a noninvertible map by an invertible history system.
2. Projective limits / Kolmogorov extension: consistent finite-dimensional distributions can define a global stochastic process under standard hypotheses.
3. Backward-state inference: smoothing, retrodiction, data assimilation, and hidden-state inference condition past trajectories on later observations.

## Novelty ceiling

WR-IV may claim no novelty for those bodies of mathematics. A defensible WREH contribution must instead come from separating world-completion multiplicity from past-history multiplicity, separating hidden-past from observable-past multiplicity, proving a precise no-rewriting/set-refinement statement, and identifying useful past/future asymmetries under declared dynamics.

## Immediate proof-risk items

- The doubling-map Cantor-fibre statement is standard and suitable only as a benchmark.
- Any use of “past” requires an explicitly declared temporal model.
- Construction/inference order must not be identified with physical time.
- A cosmological interpretation is premature at WR-IV v0.1.
