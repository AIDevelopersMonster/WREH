## Mathematical findings

### Exact layer

- finite response images form a surjective inverse system;
- the finite-consistency space is canonically identified with full profiles whose every finite restriction is realizable;
- the full WR-I response quotient embeds canonically into this space;
- the residual complement defines a model-relative global-realizability defect;
- for a finite full protocol family, the exact finite/global defect collapses identically;
- finite-support binary worlds provide an explicit infinite-protocol defect;
- finite-projection closure gives a precise completion operator;
- closed full-response image implies no exact defect;
- compact world class + Hausdorff response spaces + continuous responses imply exact globalization;
- countable nested-fibre and shrinking-diameter criteria give existence and uniqueness.

### Context layer

- restricted context families are formally separated from the full finite-bundle construction;
- a context-consistency space and contextual realizability defect are defined;
- the even-parity example gives a finite context-local/global obstruction without contradicting finite-family collapse.

### Quantitative / noisy layer

For declared protocol metrics and positive scales, WR-II defines:
- finite fitting radius delta_fin;
- one-world global fitting radius delta_glob;
- stability gap Gamma = delta_glob - delta_fin.

Proved:
- delta_fin <= delta_glob;
- if the full protocol family is finite, delta_fin = delta_glob;
- uniform equivalence of scale systems controls both radii multiplicatively and preserves zero/nonzero status;
- on compact world spaces with lower-semicontinuous residuals, delta_fin = delta_glob and the global infimum is attained;
- a finite compact-core/coercivity condition is enough; global compactness is not necessary;
- finite-support binary worlds give a positive gap example under unit scales;
- unbounded reweighting gives a zero-radius but unattained non-realizable profile.

## Important repairs made during audit

1. Nested-fibre existence now assumes closed fibres directly; the continuous-response corollary adds Hausdorff response spaces to justify closed point fibres.
2. The noisy layer is explicitly not called an approximate inverse system. Classical approximate inverse systems relax bonding-map commutativity; WR-II keeps the inverse system exact and places approximation in response residuals.
3. Semi-infinite programming is added as prior art for infinite-constraint min-max optimization.
4. Quantitative contextuality is added as prior art for robustness measures in empirical contextuality.
5. The finite-family collapse is stated in both exact and quantitative layers.
6. Protocol calibration dependence is explicit; only uniformly equivalent scale changes preserve the zero/nonzero character of the radii.

## Novelty boundary

WR-II does not claim novelty for:
- compactness;
- inverse/projective limits;
- Kolmogorov or Vorob'ev extension principles;
- contextual/global-section obstructions;
- semi-infinite programming;
- approximate inverse systems;
- robustness measures of contextuality.

The defensible contribution is architectural: attaching exact and quantitative finite/global realizability diagnostics to the WR-I world-response structure and separating full finite consistency from restricted-context consistency.

## Claim ceiling

No result establishes:
- absolute physical nonexistence outside the declared world class;
- a unique ontology;
- time, dynamics, or RG interpretation of the index/refinement order;
- that a formal completion point is a physical world;
- that a positive stability gap is physically meaningful without justified protocol metrics/scales.
