## Result provenance

Theorem 3.1 is an elementary finite-dimensional quantum speed-limit argument using an operator norm after centering the spectrum. Corollary 3.2 is its saturating qubit witness. Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2 are direct Pauli-exponential calculations. Theorem 5.1 is a restricted identification example, not a general Hamiltonian-learning method. Proposition 6.1 is monotonicity plus a witness. Proposition 7.1 is the Bernoulli Hoeffding certificate with supplied calibration error. None is presented as a discovery of quantum speed limits, tomography or concentration.

The WREH-specific contribution is an explicitly typed completion interface connecting physical resource feasibility to unresolved generator parameters and protocol refinement. This does not establish theorem priority or novelty of the broad framework. A stronger originality claim would need full-text reading and a broader source review.
