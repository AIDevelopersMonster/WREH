## Anti-duplication and novelty limits

- WR-I owns generic response equivalence and identifiable quantities; WR-VI uses a new constrained transition carrier and explicit constraint record.
- WR-II owns the finite/global extension problem; all infeasibility examples here already concern a finite bundle.
- WR-III owns generic response-fibre geometry; the scalar activity calculation is a worked constrained example, not a replacement wall theory.
- WR-IV owns history projections and smoothing; WR-VI performs neither.
- WR-V owns outcome/transition distinction and independent-row identification. WR-VI explicitly couples rows through reversibility, a support graph and a hard cap.
- WR-VII owns energy–time frontier obligations. Dimensionless one-step activity is neither energy nor a dimensional speed bound. WR-VIII/IX remain planned.

Positivity, reversible flows, linear certificates, affine intervals and nested-cap quantifiers are elementary/classical. No mathematical priority is claimed. A targeted novelty review of inverse Markov problems and activity-constrained reconstruction would be needed before a stronger research-originality claim. The present source check is not an exhaustive literature search or independent external peer review.
