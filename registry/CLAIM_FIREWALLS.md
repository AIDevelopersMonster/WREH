# WREH Claim Firewalls

WREH does not assume or claim, without an independent bridge or proof, that:

- response-equivalent realizations are ontologically identical;
- underlying reality does not exist;
- observation creates a physical world;
- experiment rewrites the past;
- the Universe literally branches;
- a collapse interpretation or many-worlds interpretation is correct;
- physical time is identical to protocol/refinement order;
- Planck scale is the limit of world existence;
- a mathematical consistency result proves physical existence;
- finite-dimensional Boundary Compensation gaps, walls, fibres, or parameter flows are directly physical energy, spacetime, cosmological boundaries, or dynamics.

WR-I claim ceiling: abstract response-defined global realization spaces, exact response equivalence, identifiability, gauge separation, protocol refinement, and finite-resolution separation under stated assumptions.

WR-VI claim ceiling: finite classical constraint-conditioned transition completion. A conservation support rule requires complete conserved labels and a justified closed-system step. Detailed balance is a supplied assumption and is not inferred from stationarity. Activity is a dimensionless one-step probability, not energy, entropy production or a rate. Hard-bound selection is conditional on independent evidence for the bound; optimization preference is not identification. A robust intersection is not the projected class for one unknown actual constraint. Strict assumptions can remove threshold attainment. No physical, causal, quantum, gravitational or cosmological wall is certified by the finite benchmark.

WR-VII claim ceiling: calibrated closed pure unitary response completion with Hamiltonian spectral diameter and evolution duration. The energy cap is neither mean energy nor work; apparatus calibration is not supplied by an SI unit convention. Qubit sufficiency uses freely available transverse controls. Revival aliases and generator circles are retained. Final-state identification differs from Hamiltonian identification; intermediate probes are separate preparations within a constant frontier family. Fixed-iid coverage does not cover adaptive stopping or drift. No universal energy/lifetime, Planck/gravity/cosmological boundary or complete-world selection follows.

WR-VIII claim ceiling: first-branch FLRW kinematic distance/rate response completion on a finite redshift interval. Standard Hogg/CBL identities and prior ruler/candle/clock calibration work (Heavens/Jimenez/Verde, 2014) and elementary inversion, scaling, interval, quotient and bump arguments are attributed and self-contained, without priority certification. Exact D and D′, independently calibrated rates and a declared joint Cartesian box of deterministic rate errors are supplied inputs. Marginal intervals alone need not certify a correlated joint constraint. SN/BAO ratios with free M and rd do not supply an absolute scale. A background singleton does not identify global topology, fields or future; a model-relative empty fibre is not universal physical impossibility. The torus witness restricts all used paths and copied classical fields to the observed patch; it does not preserve undeclared global spectra or quantum states. Kinematic future lifts need not solve one prescribed matter law. No real cosmological fit, complete CMB/lensing/growth engine, new gravity theory, spacetime edge, structural wall or universal epistemic horizon follows. WR-IX remains planned. Author-reported VII publication is separate from pending record/file verification and does not publish VIII.
