# WREH Claim Firewalls

WREH does not assume or claim, without an independent bridge or proof, that:

- response-equivalent realizations are ontologically identical;
- underlying reality does not exist;
- observation creates a physical world;
- experiment rewrites the past;
- the Universe literally branches;
- a collapse interpretation or many-worlds interpretation is correct;
- physical time is identical to protocol/refinement order;
- Planck scale is the limit of world existence;
- a mathematical consistency result proves physical existence;
- finite-dimensional Boundary Compensation gaps, walls, fibres, or parameter flows are directly physical energy, spacetime, cosmological boundaries, or dynamics.

WR-I claim ceiling: abstract response-defined global realization spaces, exact response equivalence, identifiability, gauge separation, protocol refinement, and finite-resolution separation under stated assumptions.

WR-VI claim ceiling: finite classical constraint-conditioned transition completion. A conservation support rule requires complete conserved labels and a justified closed-system step. Detailed balance is a supplied assumption and is not inferred from stationarity. Activity is a dimensionless one-step probability, not energy, entropy production or a rate. Hard-bound selection is conditional on independent evidence for the bound; optimization preference is not identification. A robust intersection is not the projected class for one unknown actual constraint. Strict assumptions can remove threshold attainment. No physical, causal, quantum, gravitational or cosmological wall is certified by the finite benchmark.
