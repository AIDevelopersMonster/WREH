## New obligation

Construct and audit restrictions that couple the experimental transition descriptions inherited from WR-V. Separate measured responses, structural assumptions, independently justified hard bounds and uncertainty about those bounds. A boundary of a constrained model is not automatically a boundary of physical existence.

The first finite classical benchmark imposes detailed balance on all rows of a three-state transition matrix. Identical prepared-input mean responses leave an interval of matrices. A stationary activity cap makes the class empty, unique or nonunique; an additional irreducibility requirement removes the exact threshold singleton. Positive response tolerance and uncertainty about the cap have distinct, explicitly proved effects.

## Claim ceiling

All results are finite-model statements using established positivity, Markov-chain and linear-feasibility tools. No mathematical priority, physical implementation, universal cap, entropy-production law, causal speed limit, quantum theorem, gravitational wall or cosmological conclusion is claimed. Activity is a dimensionless one-step probability, not energy or a rate. Projected transition uniqueness is not unique full-world identification.
