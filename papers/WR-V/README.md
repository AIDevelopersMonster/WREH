## New obligation

An outcome likelihood does not in general determine the state transition associated with an experiment. WR-V declares an outcome-plus-successor instrument and asks which calibrated additional responses identify its compatible transition descriptions.

The new object is the instrument-completion class, first at fixed outcome likelihoods and then at fixed successor-probe responses. This is not a new response quotient, existence theorem, generic fibre geometry or smoothing method.

## Scope and four scenarios

BRANCH requires a declared outcome tree or successor-support model; it does not establish physical branching. CONTEXTUALIZE specifies how variables are identified between contexts. SELECT and REMAIN A CLASS concern the cardinality of a named compatible class. The four labels are not mutually exclusive or an exhaustive classification of ontologies.

The draft supplies:

1. A finite classical instrument, its outcome marginal, and distinct input/successor posterior images.
2. The product-of-simplices ambiguity left by known outcome likelihoods.
3. A calibrated transition inverse problem, with uniform rank and pointwise feasible-direction certificates.
4. A three-state benchmark showing that pure-state separation need not identify mixtures; a second detector completes the finite transition law.
5. Explicit finite-resolution failure of exact boundary uniqueness and a reconstruction error bound.
6. An abstract contextual parity example and a finite adaptive positive-likelihood non-selection lemma.

The arguments are elementary/classical and have no mathematical priority claim. No real experiment or quantum implementation is reported. A unique projected label or transition law is not a unique full world without an injective bridge.
