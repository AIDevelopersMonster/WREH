One anchor

A squared-range response fixes a circle. The inverse image is one-dimensional and non-rigid.

Two anchors

The circle ambiguity collapses to a mirror pair. On the baseline the two branches merge: a fold/bifurcation wall.

Three non-collinear anchors

The reflection ambiguity is removed. The response map is an embedding into its intrinsic image, so every fibre is a singleton.

Reviewer's requested bridge

WR-II supplies conditions under which the response-compatible inverse image is nonempty. WR-III assumes nonemptiness and studies the geometry and topology of that set-theoretic preimage. The manuscript uses the word fibre in this broad inverse-image sense before any bundle structure is assumed.
