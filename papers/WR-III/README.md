# WR-III — Geometry of Admissible World Fibres

**Full title:** Geometry of Admissible World Fibres: Refinement, Rigidity and Realizability Walls  
**Programme:** World Realizability & Epistemic Horizons (WREH)  
**Upstream:** WR-I DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23210258; WR-II DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23224154  
**Status:** PUBLICATION_READY / PREPRINT CANDIDATE v0.6

## New mathematical target

WR-I defined response equivalence and response-defined world spaces. WR-II supplied exact and quantitative criteria for global realizability. WR-III assumes a nonempty response-compatible inverse image and studies its geometry under protocol refinement.

WR-III assumes a response value is realizable and studies the geometry of its admissible world fibre:
\[
\Phi_{\mathcal A}(e):=R_{\mathcal A}^{-1}(e),
\qquad e\in E_{\mathcal A}=R_{\mathcal A}(X).
\]

Here \(X\) is the declared geometric realization carrier. It may be the original world class or a justified gauge-reduced carrier; no quotient is assumed automatically.

## Core questions

1. When does the fibre correspondence vary upper/lower semicontinuously with response data?
2. Which fibre-size observables are stable?
3. How does protocol refinement shrink fibres?
4. When does refinement produce exact or finite-resolution rigidity?
5. Which response values lie on structural walls where local fibre type changes?
6. How do attainable-response boundaries differ from internal singularity walls?

## First exact results targeted

- refinement inclusion of fibres;
- monotonicity of fibre diameter under refinement;
- upper hemicontinuity of inverse fibres for continuous maps from compact carriers;
- equivalence between openness of the response map and lower hemicontinuity of the inverse fibre correspondence;
- upper semicontinuity of fibre diameter on compact metric carriers;
- openness of strict epsilon-rigidity loci;
- Ehresmann-type local triviality away from proper smooth critical values;
- separation of attainable-response boundary from internal structural wall.

## Claim ceiling

WR-III does **not** claim that:

- a response fibre is an ontological ensemble of actually existing universes;
- small fibre diameter is a universal physical notion without a declared metric;
- a structural wall is a physical wall of spacetime or of the Universe;
- refinement order is physical time, dynamics, RG flow, or causal evolution;
- critical values of a response map are phase transitions without a separate physical bridge;
- BC walls/fibres/atlases transfer automatically into WREH.

## Anti-duplication boundary

WR-III does not re-prove:
- WR-I response equivalence;
- WR-II finite/global existence criteria;
- WR-II residual radii and stability gap.

Its new object is the variation of nonempty inverse fibres over realizable response space.
