WR-IV: Response-Conditioned Global Completion and the Status of the Past. Technical preprint v0.6 by A. A. Malachevsky (WREH), with a complete English manuscript and a parallel complete Russian translation.

The paper distinguishes compatible global completions, projected histories, observable records, conditional probability laws, supports and covariance. It gives an explicit WR-II realizability gate, a sufficient condition for transferring WR-III structural walls, a deterministic doubling-map benchmark, and classical Gaussian and Brownian smoothing benchmarks. A weakly continuous regular conditional Brownian-bridge kernel is constructed for every endpoint value. The work demonstrates that a fixed earlier prefix can retain its entire compatibility set while its conditional variance decreases. Independent Gaussian measurement noise preserves path-measure equivalence, whereas bounded admissible noise constrains the endpoint. The classical Rauch–Tung–Striebel recursion is specialized to a fully specified random-walk model, with a two-observation calculation and independent reproducibility checks.

The contribution is an explicitly declared WREH comparison framework and a careful separation of mathematical objects. Set-image statements are elementary and the stochastic/topological benchmarks are classical; no new theorem priority or smoothing method is claimed. The paper does not infer physical rewriting of the past, retrocausality, ontological branching or the emergence of physical time. This is an unpeer-reviewed expository technical preprint; comprehensive originality certification is not asserted.

The package contains both PDFs, common bilingual LaTeX source, analytic figures, an optional standalone offline English/Russian HTML demonstration, reproducibility scripts, source map and audit.
