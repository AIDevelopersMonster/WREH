Finite Consistency ≠ Global Realizability

Two minimal models illustrate the distinction between finite realizability and a single global realization. The first needs infinitely many protocols; the second uses a restricted family of jointly admissible contexts.

Finite-support world class

Worlds are infinite binary sequences with only finitely many 1s. Protocol Pn reads coordinate n.

Every finite binary pattern is realizable: extend it by zeros. But the infinite all-ones profile is not a finite-support world.
